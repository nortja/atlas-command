# Application data-handling disclosure — draft for review
Publisher requested: Jaison Norton. Not yet a published privacy policy.

The inspected prototype source stores the workspace and attached media locally. It contains no application analytics SDK, advertising SDK, remote AI endpoint or cloud-workspace synchronization. Local IPC uses a loopback-only server and a private Edge profile.

The user can deliberately import files, export records, attach media, open evidence links and open attached PDFs. External websites and external viewers have their own practices; Microsoft Store, Windows and Edge also operate under their own terms and settings. Application-local storage does not mean that the operating system, endpoint-management tools, device backups or browser runtime never communicate externally.

Exports may contain private notes and precise coordinates. The owner chooses when and with whom to share them. Media files are separate from JSON exports. Data location and retention through uninstall must be tested for the packaged release; maintain a complete backup.

This draft does not claim legal compliance, encryption at rest, automatic anonymization, anonymous Store usage or confirmed uninstall behavior. The exact packaged build, support contact, effective date and any portal-required published policy URL remain to be finalized before submission.
