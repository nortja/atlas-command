# Camera and refresh disclosure — draft for the consolidated release

Incorporate this into the complete policy before submission. It is not a standalone final privacy policy or Store approval.

Optional public snapshots: after you allow the listed USGS connections and select Load snapshot, the local helper requests one of three registered public HTTPS JPEG paths. USGS receives your connection IP, the product user-agent and camera path, not your selected research site, notes, media or account details in the request. The adapter supplies no authentication credentials or cookies and does not follow redirects. Temporary pictures are retained in process memory, not recorded into the workspace. Memory copies can remain until replaced, consent is next checked as disabled, or the helper exits. Closing the view removes its displayed image; an in-flight request may finish before cancellation.

Favorites, selected source and preferences are local workspace settings and can appear in exports/backups. Optional automatic snapshot refresh operates approximately every five minutes after explicit viewing starts and while the panel/app is visible. Pause/close/hide stops repeat requests. Capture time can differ from retrieval time; source timestamps and credits remain visible. Retrieval success alone does not establish current conditions.

NASA and National Park Service continuous-video sources are official external page links in this implementation. Opening them contacts those services and their third-party players under their own privacy/audio/cookie practices. Atlas does not embed or record that continuous video. Public access does not confer commercial reuse rights to all linked material.

Weather refresh can be Balanced, Hourly local, Daily or Manual while Atlas is running and visible. Existing Open-Meteo coordinate/IP disclosures apply. Daily mode may display stale data with a warning. Data refresh never installs downloaded application scripts, Windows services or background scheduled tasks. Software updates remain separate Store releases.

The requested private Field Camera is not yet connected or recording. Its complete disclosure and permission design must be added only after its actual device/transport/capture behavior is implemented and verified.
