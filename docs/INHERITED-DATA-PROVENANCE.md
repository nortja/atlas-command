# Starter data provenance

This prototype has 26 starter records: 22 archaeological sites and 4 generalized LiDAR landscape entries. It does **not** contain the complete 307-entry v3.1 atlas. The full original remains unchanged. Import the latest master CSV to bring the remaining records into this application. Duplicate name/coordinate records are skipped rather than overwriting your notes.

The starter records are selected and paraphrased from the user’s Ancient Maps atlas-master-v3.1.csv, atlas-master-v3.0.csv, and earlier catalog excerpts retrieved September 8, 2026. Source URLs and inherited mystery ratings are preserved for review, not independently re-certified. A mystery value is not evidence strength or discovery probability. The previous five-factor rating rubric needs a row-by-row audit before scores should be called recalculated.

No stock photos, synthetic site photos, live feeds, new site discoveries, magnetic prediction scores, or travel advisories are fabricated. Attach locally verified photographs and documents to individual records. LiDAR points are deliberately generalized landscape references; they do not identify hidden ruins for field navigation.

Modern low-resolution globe geometry is extracted from the installed countryinfo 0.1.2 geoJSON data; its MIT license is included. Geometry is for an overview, not historical coastlines, political-border adjudication, road routing, or navigation. The star field, orbital rings and atmosphere are decorative.
