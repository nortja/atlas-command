## Short description

## Description
Atlas Command brings a rotating world view, a personal site library and flexible information panels into one exploration workspace. Move between Observatory and Mission views, select locations, add your own notes and local media, and organize potential visits without sending your workspace to an application cloud account.

This initial edition supports point-site imports from CSV and KML/KMZ, a record-filtering timeline, a manual magnetic-angle comparison tool, and an expedition notebook with costs entered by the user. Archaeology and LiDAR records share a common structure with personal mineral and fossil interests.

Scientific and travel limitations are labeled in the interface. Decorative stars and atmospheric effects are not live observations. There is no automatic lost-site prediction, live weather feed, real-time travel advisory service, raw LiDAR viewer or booking integration in this edition. No discoveries, safe travel or collecting permissions are guaranteed.
